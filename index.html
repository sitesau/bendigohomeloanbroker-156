Key Questions to Ask Before Applying

With numerous lenders, each possessing distinct rates, policies, and specific requirements, a broker's role is to narrow down this broad field into a manageable shortlist for your consideration. It's vital to understand the rationale behind each lender recommendation, what options were ruled out, and what assumptions could potentially alter the proposed solution.

  • Deposit and Equity: Enquire how your available funds influence lender selection, potential mortgage insurance costs, and the overall confidence in loan approval.
  • Loan Purpose: Clearly state whether the loan is for purchasing a home, refinancing, funding a renovation, acquiring an investment property, or securing pre-approval.
  • Repayment Style: Ask about the relevance of fixed, variable, offset, and interest-only repayment features to your specific financial situation and goals.
  • Timing: Understand the necessary steps and timelines leading up to finance approval, formal approval, and the final settlement.
  • Disclosure: Clarify whether any borrower fees apply and how lender commissions will be transparently presented.

Compliance and Consumer Protection

Mortgage brokers operating in Australia are regulated under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009. Professionals in this field must hold an Australian Credit Licence or operate as Credit Representatives under a licensed entity. Many brokers are accredited with industry bodies such as the Mortgage and Finance Association of Australia (MFAA) or the Finance Brokers Association of Australia (FBAA). Furthermore, partner brokers typically have access to external dispute resolution services through the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA).

The obligations placed on brokers by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) are fundamental. Brokers are legally required to act in the consumer's best interests and prioritise the consumer's needs when providing credit assistance. Key questions to ask include who holds the relevant credit licence or representative appointment, which dispute resolution body is applicable, how the broker's recommendation will be documented, and when you can expect to receive disclosure of any commissions or fees.
